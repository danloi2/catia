## Introduction

**How do you believe Artificial Intelligence can transform your teaching practice?**

This 3-hour practical workshop explores Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its application in teaching, covering everything from ethical and technological foundations to the creation of effective prompts for optimizing education.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, educators will:

* **Understand** the basic principles and technical foundations of AI.
* **Explore** pedagogical tools and AI-driven practices.
* **Reflect** on the ethics of AI use in the classroom.
* **Integrate** AI meaningfully and responsibly into their professional work.

---

## Learning Itinerary

The workshop follows a structured path (Total: 180 minutes):

| Phase | Activity | Description | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | **First Steps** | Introduction and workshop motivation. | 20' |
| **1** | **Task 1: AI CREDO** | Exploration of AI foundations. | 30' |
| **2** | **Task 2: AI LITURGY** | Discovering AI tools and practices. | 30' |
| **3** | **Task 3: AI DEEDS** | Reflection on ethical AI usage for teachers. | 30' |
| **4** | **Final Task: AI PRAYER** | Creating and testing AI prompts for teaching. | 30' |
| **5** | **Self-Assessment** | Final reflection on learning and utility. | 10' |

---

## Final Product

The practical outcome of this workshop will be:
**Designing and testing an AI Prompt specifically applicable to your area of knowledge.**
